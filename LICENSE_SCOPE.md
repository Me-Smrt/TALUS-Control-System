## What these licenses mean

- **MPL-2.0 (software):** You may study, use, modify, and share the covered code, including commercially. Distributed modifications to covered files generally remain under the MPL with applicable source and notices available.
- **CERN-OHL-W-2.0 (hardware):** You may study, manufacture, modify and share the covered board designs, including commercially. The license has notice-preservation and complete-source obligations for covered hardware designs and their distributed modifications. Components with their own rights retain those rights.

The full licenses, rather than this friendly overview, govern permitted uses. In particular, the CERN license contains a patent-license grant in section 7; review that provision before public release if you are considering patent protection.
