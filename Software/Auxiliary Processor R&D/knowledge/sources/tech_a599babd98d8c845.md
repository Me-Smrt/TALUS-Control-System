Where to Park on Campus

Navigating parking on the university campus can be a challenge. To streamline this
 process, the university has a two color-coded parking permit system. Purchasing a
 virtual permit may enable you to park in multiple parking zones; check the breakdown
 of permit colors and their matching zones below to see what zones your virtual permit
 enables you to park in. If you have a virtual student permit, for example, you can
 park in purple parking zones.

If you have any questions about virtual parking permit colors or parking zones, let us know!

Parking Permit Zones

 Employee Parking

 Can park in gold and purple spaces.

 Student Parking

 Can park in purple spaces.

 Accessible Parking

 Can park in any legal accessible parking space, excluding reserved, visitor and campus-operated vehicle parking.

 Motorcycle Parking

 Can park in motorcycle parking spots only.
