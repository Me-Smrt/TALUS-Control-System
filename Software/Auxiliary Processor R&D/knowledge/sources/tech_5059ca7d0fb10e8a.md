Whitney Ave. parking lot has reopened

Students, faculty and staff:

We are pleased to share that more than 90% of the purple-zoned parking lot bound by
 North Whitney Avenue and North Willow Avenue has reopened for student and employee
 use! 

Members of the campus community with a valid parking permit can begin using this lot
 immediately.

Please note there is a temporary turnaround at the north end of the parking lot as
 construction personnel continue some work in this area that is tied to the J.J. Oakley
 Innovation Center & Residence Hall.

Access to this lot is available via Wings Up Way. The West 10th Street access point
 remains temporarily closed due to construction.

As a reminder, you can receive timely parking messages like these on your mobile device
 by downloading the Passio GO for free in the Apple Store or on Google Play.

Once the app is installed, go to “My Agency” on the left-hand side of the app and
 search “Tennessee Tech,” then click to select. By enabling push notifications, users
 can receive a message on their phone’s lock screen any time a new parking message
 is shared.

Thank you for your patience as we have completed the paving, striping and lighting
 installation in this lot to better serve you!

Improvements coming to Stadium Drive at West 12th Street 

Students, faculty and staff:

Beginning Monday, Sept. 28, the university will perform needed repairs at the intersection
 of West 12th Street and Stadium Drive. This will include new drainage to address standing
 water along Stadium Drive, as well as a new crosswalk, sidewalk, and paving. 

To complete these repairs, the entrance to Stadium Drive from West 12th Street will
 temporarily close, and a separate entrance will open just west of that intersection,
 which will lead directly into the purple-zoned parking lot between the Hooper Eblen
 Center and Tucker Stadium. This is shown on the map above. 

No parking spaces are impacted by this repair work. 

Thank you for your understanding as we make these improvements to campus to better
 serve you.

Temporary partial closure of Golden Eagle Circle 

Students, faculty and staff:

Please be advised that part of Golden Eagle Circle, stretching from the Dixie Avenue
 entrance to the edge of North Hall (shown in the image above), will be closed to vehicular
 traffic beginning Thursday, Sept. 17 to allow for repairs to an underground steam
 line. Pedestrian walkways will not be affected by the closure.

During this time, vehicles accessing the purple-zoned parking lot behind North Hall
 and Jobe and Murphy Halls must enter via the lot entrance located north of Jobe and
 Murphy Halls along Dixie Avenue. Three exit points from the lot will remain available
 throughout the road closure.

Thank you for your understanding as we work to complete these necessary repairs as
 quickly as possible.

Parking and traffic pattern changes for ACME construction

Students, faculty and staff:

At the end of June, we reached out to let campus know of upcoming parking and traffic
 pattern changes near the site of the future Advanced Construction and Manufacturing
 Engineering (ACME) Building.

While inclement weather temporarily delayed some of these changes, please be advised
 that the parking closures originally slated to begin June 29 will now take effect
 June 16, 2026. 

Please ensure that all vehicles are cleared from the shaded area in the above image
 by June 16, 2026.

Beginning tomorrow, a portion of the red-zoned parking lot near the Millard Oakley
 STEM Center and the green-zoned parking lot bound by Lab Science Commons, Evins Hall
 and Southwest Hall will close until fall 2028. 
​
Additionally, the small portion of Wings Up Way near Stadium Drive (shown in the image
 above) will be closed to vehicular traffic. A pedestrian pathway will be established
 to allow unobstructed access to the Marc L. Burnett Student Recreation & Fitness Center. 

Capital Projects will keep roughly half of the STEM Center parking lot open throughout
 construction, with a one-way entrance and exit traffic pattern.

Thank you for your understanding as we make these improvements to campus.

Wings Up!

What we are doing now!

Tech’s Office of Parking and Transportation, in conjunction with campus facilities
 staff, are actively working to expand and rezone select parking lots in order to minimize
 the impact on campus parking.

Campus Shuttle

We've added a new Wednesday evening stop downtown so students can enjoy local shops
 and restaurants.  The campus shuttle route and stop locations are available in the
 PassioGo! app. You can also find this information in each shuttle shelter, on every
 shuttle bus, and on the Parking and Transportation webpage. 

Visitor Parking

The process for obtaining a visitor permit has been streamlined. Campus departments
 can issue their visitors a Departmental Guest Invite, and guests can use their smartphone
 and the QR code posted at visitor parking spaces to obtain a day-use visitor permit.

Parking Garage

The initial design and review of the proposed parking garage received approval from
 the Board of Trustees at their December 2025 meeting. Updates will be shared as more
 information becomes available. 

In the spring of 2024,  the Student Government Association (SGA) conducted a Transportation,
 Parking, and Infrastructure (TPI) Fee Student Survey. It has now closed. With 32%
 of respondents in favor and 68% not in favor of the proposed fee restructure, the
 proposal detailed in the survey will not be implemented for the next term. Thank you
 for voicing your opinion in this poll, and we hope to use your input and to inform
 future parking proposals.

Future Updates

Tech is committed to keeping you updated with information you can use to minimize
 the impact of these changes on your daily commute.  Please check back regularly to
 see what these changes are.
