Event Parking

Weekday Events

Parking and Transportation Services is committed to providing positive parking experience
 for students, faculty, staff, and visitors. To help ensure parking remains readily
 available during peak class times, departments are encouraged to schedule events in
 the late afternoon or evening whenever possible.

For events held during class hours, we recommend using the UCHRA shuttle service to
 transport guests between designated parking areas and the event location. This convenient
 option helps guests reach campus easily while supporting parking availability for
 the campus community. The estimated cost for shuttle service is approximately $60
 per hour.

 Guest Parking Options

Hyder-Burks Pavilion (Preferred Location)

2392 Gainesboro Grade

This location offers several advantages:

Preserves on-campus parking spaces during busy class hours.

Guests do not need to register their vehicles or obtain a temporary parking permit.

Provides a convenient park-and-ride option when paired with the UCHRA shuttle service.

Purple Lot (Alternative Location)

350 University Drive

Guests using this lot must register their vehicle and obtain a temporary parking permit
 before parking.

Parking and Transportation Services appreciates your partnership in helping us maintain
 a welcoming and accessible campus. We encourage departments to consider parking arrangements
 early in the event planning process and to contact us by emailing parking@tntech.edu or call 931-372-6428 with any questions or special accommodations.

Evening and Weekend Events

For events held after 4:30 p.m. on weekdays, visitors may park in any available non-reserved
 parking space without a virtual parking permit.

In addition, virtual parking permits are not required from Friday at 4:30 p.m. until
 Monday at 7:30 a.m., making evening and weekend events especially convenient for guests.

Please note that regulations for reserved spaces and illegal parking remain in effect
 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to ensure safety and access for all campus users.
