Wings up Means… 
 Embracing the Challenge.

 Tech is known for our exceptional STEM programs like engineering and computer science,
 but we offer more than 200 academic programs – each with outstanding faculty ready
 to help you forge the path to the future you want.

 Agriculture & Human Ecology

 The College of Agriculture and Human Ecology develops skills in food production, dietetics,
 families, farming, pre-veterinary science, and design and merchandising.

 Arts & Sciences

 The College of Arts and Sciences provides core courses and a liberal arts education
 that fosters lifelong learning and critical thinking across diverse careers.

 Business

 The College of Business prepares leaders through programs in management, marketing,
 finance, accounting, IT, economics, international business, and graduate studies.

 Education & Human Sciences

 The College of Education & Human Sciences offers programs in teaching, counseling,
 psychology, allied health, and more, with strong support and research opportunities.

 Emerging & Integrative Studies

 The College of Emerging and Integrative Studies offers custom degrees for students
 pursuing specialized studies not covered by other programs.

 Engineering

 The College of Engineering grants 400+ degrees annually across seven disciplines and
 offers graduate and doctoral programs.

 Fine Arts

 Find the inspiration to transform your passion and skills into mature artistry in the
 College of Fine Arts.

 Whitson-Hester College of Nursing

 A diverse community for first-time nursing students, career changers, and RNs advancing
 to BSN or MSN degrees.
