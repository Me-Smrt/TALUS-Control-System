Welcome to Tennessee Tech

Whether you are a visitor, alumnus, student or faculty/staff member, we are glad to
 see you on our campus. Parking & Transportation, a division of Auxiliary Services,
 oversees parking permits, regulations, and citations, as well as the campus shuttle.
 Our goal is to create a positive and supportive parking and transportation experience
 by offering clear communication, courteous service, and practical solutions. We are
 committed to enhance mobility, accessibility, and sustainability through a range of
 thoughtful transportation options that serve the entire University community.

 Parking Enforcement: 

Fall and Spring Semesters: Parking attendants enforce regulations in all parking lots while classes are in
 session. 

Summer Semester: Parking attendants enforce regulations in the employee only parking lots and reserved
 parking lots throughout the summer semester, including during intersession classes.

Year-round: Parking attendants enforce accessible, reserved, and visitor spaces 24/7, seven
 days a week.
