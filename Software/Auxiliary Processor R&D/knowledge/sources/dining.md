# 2026-2027 Meal Plans

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: student_life.

For 2026-2027, residential meal plans include Gold Unlimited at $3,557 per semester and Purple Weekly at $3,443 per semester. Freshmen living in residence halls are required to purchase a meal plan for their first two semesters, excluding summer, and may select a residential option; if no selection is made, the site says Purple Weekly is the default. Commuter-oriented block plans are also offered. Dining dollars and unused meals generally have term-specific expiration rules, so current terms should be checked before giving financial guidance.
