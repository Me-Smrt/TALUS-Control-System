Spring Family Day is Saturday, February 21, 2026!

02/21/2026 at 08:00 AM

 Discover Cookeville

Take some time to explore your student's home away from home! Enjoy a bite to eat
 at one of Cookeville's local restaurants, some shopping in historic downtown, or go
 for a hike! There's plenty to do both in and near the Cookeville area. Check out visitcookevilletn.com
 for some great ideas!

 Parent Association Local Meet-Up

 among the Parent Association Board of Trustees and other Parent Association Members

 Women's Basketball vs. Lindenwood
