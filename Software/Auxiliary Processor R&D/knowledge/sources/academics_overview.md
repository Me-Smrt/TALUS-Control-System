# Academics Overview

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: academics.

Tennessee Tech says it offers more than 200 academic programs spanning undergraduate, master’s, doctoral, and Education Specialist levels. Academic areas include longstanding programs in liberal arts, education, engineering, nursing and newer or expanding areas such as cybersecurity, environmental studies, business and design. The university highlights experiential learning across undergraduate programs and a technology-infused general education foundation.
