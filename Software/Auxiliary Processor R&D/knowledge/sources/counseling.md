# Counseling and Mental Health Wellness

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: health_safety.

The Center for Counseling and Mental Health Wellness provides brief, short-term, solution-focused services to Tennessee Tech students. Office: Roaden University Center Room 307, 1000 N. Dixie Ave.; Monday-Friday 8 a.m.-4:30 p.m. Central. Phone 931-372-3331; email counsel@tntech.edu. The Eagle Eye Crisis Hotline is available at 855-206-8997 or 931-372-3331. For immediate danger, call 911. Do not rely on email for urgent mental-health situations.
