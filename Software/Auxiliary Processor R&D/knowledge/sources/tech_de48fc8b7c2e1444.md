Graduation Application Basics

» How do I apply online for graduation?

The application is available in Eagle Online. View the steps for submitting an application.

NOTE: To have your name appear in the printed Commencement Program, your online registration
 request must be submitted by the official submission deadline.

If a Nondisclosure of Directory Information request is on file, the University will
 not release the candidate’s name for any commencement publications that are made available
 to the public. This can include: proofs of gradation photos, hometown news releases,
 commencement program, etc. The candidate can request the removal of the nondisclosure
 request by logging into Eagle Online and removing the “Confidential Flag” on the student
 record.

» How do I know when to apply?

Plan ahead and have conversations as early as possible with your advisor. Use your
 Degree Works audit to review your remaining graduation requirements and plan out your
 remaining requirements with your advisor in order to graduate your intended term.

» I'm going on co-op (internship, study abroad, etc.) When do I apply?

You will need to apply for graduation online by the published deadline. 

» Where do I enter the catalog year on my application?

By default, the online graduation application process uses the term that you entered
 the major as your catalog year. Please review your DegreeWorks audit BEFORE applying
 to confirm it is correct. If your catalog year does need to be changed, please do
 so BEFORE applying by submitting a change of major form to the Office of the Registrar.

» I keep getting an error message, "No curriculum available." Why is this?

If you get this error message, it can mean one of three things: 1) you do not have
 the required minimum of 80 earned credit hours to apply; 2) you are applying to graduate
 from a non-degree program and need to change your major (e.g. Basic Business, Pre-Medicine,
 etc.) or 3) you already have an online graduation application on file.

» How do I know if I've already applied?

Access your Eagle Online account via TechExpress and review your DegreeWorks audit.
 If you have submitted an online graduation application, it will be visible at the
 top of your degree evaluation in the student information section under your overall
 GPA.

» I'm graduating in the Summer, which commencement is for me?

Summer graduates are included in the Fall commencement ceremonies with the December
 graduates. However your diploma will be mailed out in August at the completion of
 the Summer term and not in December. If you would like special consideration to participate
 in the Spring commencement before completing classes in the summer, please fill out the online Request to Walk form on our forms page. The Undergraduate Graduation office staff will email you when a decision has been
 reached regarding your request. The message will be sent after summer applications
 have been processed.  

» I missed the graduation application deadline. Now what?

Complete the Late Application for Graduation in Eagle Online. All late applications
 are processed in the order in which they are received.

» How do I register with a graduation hold?

Email the Graduation at u_graduation@tntech.edu and request that the hold be removed.

Undergraduate Graduation Degree Analysis

» What is the degree analysis?

A degree analysis lists all remaining requirements (e.g., General Education classes,
 degree coursework, overall GPA, major GPA, etc.) needed for completion of your academic
 program. It will be uploaded to your Eagle Online after processing and both you and
 your advisor will be notified that it is ready via email.

» I've applied for graduation, how do I get my degree analysis now?

Degree audits are completed in the order in which the applications are received. You
 will receive an email in your Tech email account notifying you that it has been processed
 and uploaded into Eagle Online.

» I'm still showing requirements I've completed. Why?

Talk with your advisor and review your DegreeWorks audit. Make sure that all transfer
 credit you have earned has been applied to your transcript and that any course substitutions
 that you need have been filed by your advisor. If you still see requirements you do
 not understand, make sure that you and your advisor are looking at the correct catalog
 year for your major and have not overlooked any requirements.

 Changes to My Application

» I've applied but need to change my major. How do I do this?

Please review your DegreeWorks audit after your major change and submit a new application
 for graduation. Submit your graduation application form to the Registrar's Office in
 Jere Whitson 221 and include any forms (substitutions, emphasis area) needed in order
 to process your application. Late applications are processed after the online applications
 are completed.

» How do I move my graduation application to the next term?

If you have applied for graduation and need to change your anticipated date of graduation,
 please email the Registrar’s Office at u_graduation@tntech.edu.  Please include your name, T number, the original date of intended graduation and
 the new anticipated date.

» What if I decide not to attend commencement?

The Registrar's Office sends out an email about midway through the semester to all
 students who have applied to graduate. You will confirm at that time if you still
 plan to attend and will also provide an updated mailing address.

» What if my mailing address changes?

Graduation will send out an email message to the undergraduate candidates around mid-semester.
 You will confirm at that time if you plan to attend the ceremony and provide an updated
 mailing address for the diploma.

After Your Last Semester

» How will I receive my diploma if I didn't attend commencement?

Diplomas are mailed out. Graduation will send out an email message to the undergraduate
 candidates around mid-semester. You will confirm at that time if you plan to attend
 or not and provide a mailing address for the diploma. Please refer to the Important
 Dates & Deadlines to see when diplomas will be mailed.

» What happens if I failed a class, GPA decreased, or I dropped a required course and
 I was supposed to graduate?

You do not need to reapply. The Graduation Office will automatically move your application
 to the next term, so you will be eligible to receive your diploma the next semester.
 Contact your advisor immediately to determine how to complete your remaining requirements.

» What do I do if was suspended when I was supposed to graduate?

Contact the Registrar’s Office at u_graduation@tntech.edu to update your expected term of graduation
