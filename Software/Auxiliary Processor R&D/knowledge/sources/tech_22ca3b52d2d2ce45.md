The Tennessee Tech Promise is a scholarship that ensures more students have access
 to a Tech education. Now, one of the top universities in Tennessee that is recognized
 as having the best Return on Investment is within your reach.

As a last-dollar scholarship, Tech Promise will pay outstanding mandatory tuition
 and fees that are not met by other scholarships and grants. It will not cover specialized
 academic fees, miscellaneous fees or housing and meal plan charges. Qualified students
 are eligible up to four years.

Be Bold. Be Fearless. Be Confident. You are meant to be at Tennessee Tech University.

To qualify, students must have:

Have a total adjusted gross income of $40,000 or less as determined by the FAFSA 

Receive the Tennessee Educational Lottery HOPE Scholarship and federal Pell Grant

Be a full-time new freshmen or transfer starting in Fall 2020 and beyond
