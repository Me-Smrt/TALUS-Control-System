# Volpe Library Hours and Services

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: student_services.

During regular fall/spring semesters, Volpe Library building hours are listed as Monday-Thursday 7 a.m.-midnight, Friday 7 a.m.-6 p.m., Saturday 10 a.m.-6 p.m., and Sunday 1 p.m.-midnight. Department hours differ. The page notes 24-hour computer labs in Henderson Hall 111 and Clement Hall 313B using Eagle Card access. Emergency, weather, break and holiday schedules can alter hours.
