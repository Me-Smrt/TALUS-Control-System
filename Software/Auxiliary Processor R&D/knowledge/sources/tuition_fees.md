# 2026-2027 Tuition and Fee Overview

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: costs.

For Fall 2026, Spring 2027 and Summer 2027, the Bursar page gives example per-semester direct costs. For an in-state undergraduate taking 12 credit hours, tuition and fees are listed as $6,298 before certain course/program fees. The example regular double residence-hall room is $3,503 and the example Purple Weekly meal plan is $3,443, for an example in-state semester total of $13,244. Domestic and international out-of-state surcharges are listed separately. Graduate examples use 10 credit hours. Fees can change and some majors/courses add charges, so use the current Bursar schedule for exact billing.
