# International Undergraduate Admissions

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: admissions.

International undergraduate admissions uses a dedicated application path. The public site distinguishes international first-time and transfer applicants and notes that non-immigrant students may require immigration-related documentation. Separate requirement pages describe academic records, certified translations when records are not in English, financial documentation, and passport/visa-related materials. Because immigration and admission rules are time-sensitive, the assistant should cite the local snapshot and recommend the International Admissions office when a requirement is not clearly supported.
