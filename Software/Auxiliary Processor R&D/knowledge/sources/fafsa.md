Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: aid.

The 2027-2028 FAFSA became available September 23, 2026 for Fall 2027, Spring 2028 and Summer 2028 attendance. The 2026-2027 FAFSA applies to Fall 2026, Spring 2027 and Summer 2027.
