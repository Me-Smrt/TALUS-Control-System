Step 2: Explore TechExpress

TechExpress is a powerful resource you'll use often! You'll find all your most-used
 Tennessee Tech resources, including your Tech email, Wi-Fi password, T-Number, University
 news and events, the campus directory, and so much more. TechExpress also automatically
 logs you into all campus websites. You won't need to remember multiple usernames and
 passwords for different sites; as long as you're logged into TechExpress, you're good
 to go!

Eagle Online: This is the student portal you will use to access class schedules, financial aid,
 and other student information throughout your college career. To access Eagle Online,
 log in to TechExpress with your Tennessee Tech username and password and click the Eagle quickLink.

Tech Email: Every new student at Tech is given a Tech email account. Your email will be the primary
 communication method for Financial Aid, Scholarships, and more, so keep your eyes
 peeled for exciting updates. To access your email, log in to TechExpress (if you aren’t already logged in) and click the Mail quickLink. 

Explore TechExpress

Step 3: Apply for Scholarships! 

Tennessee Tech has a single scholarship application that automatically puts you in
 the running for over 675 scholarships, many of which are renewable for up to four
 years. Be sure to apply as early as possible!

To complete the University's scholarship application, new students must be admitted to Tennessee Tech. Tennessee Tech's scholarship application must be completed each year.

The scholarship deadlines are listed below:

New Freshmen - December 15 for the next Fall semester

Transfer Students - May 1 for the next Fall semester (January 15 priority deadline)

Applying for scholarships is done through your Tech Express portal. Once you log in to TechExpress, select the ‘ScholrWeb’ quickLink icon to access Scholar Web and begin your scholarship
 application!

How to apply for scholarships

Step 4: Submit Your FAFSA

In order to be eligible for financial aid, you must have a FAFSA on file. This is
 something you must do every year as a college student, but it is particularly important
 to ensure you get as much aid as you can in your freshman year. To automatically send
 us your FAFSA information, add Tennessee Tech to your FAFSA application using our official
 code: 003523.

If you have questions about your financial aid, email financialaid@tntech.edu.

Complete the FAFSA

Step 5: Accept Your Scholarships

Being awarded a scholarship does not mean you automatically receive it; you must either
 accept or decline the award before the deadline, given on the award letter or email,
 in order to secure it. Make sure you check your Tech email often for notices of awards.
 You can check your award status and accept scholarships by going to TechExpress and selecting the "ScholrWeb" quickLink.

If you have questions about your scholarships, email scholarships@tntech.edu.

Step 7: Review and Track Your Admit to Enrollment Checklist

Once you’ve been admitted, log in to your Application Portal to review your personalized next steps for enrollment. Your portal is where you’ll
 track important deadlines, submit any outstanding documents and complete required
 action items before classes start for your admitted term. Your Admit to Enrollment Checklist updates as you move closer to enrolling, with new tasks added when it’s time to complete
 them.

Be sure to check your Application Portal often so you don’t miss any steps for your first semester.

Access Your Application Portal

Step 8: Register for Orientation

Orientation is your next step in the enrollment process, including academic advisement
 and registering for classes. The Office of New Student & Family Programs has created
 a variety of orientation programs for new students and their families to help ease
 your transition to campus. During orientation, you will learn about campus resources,
 academics, advisement & course registration, and life as a Golden Eagle.

Orientation information for Summer/Fall admits is communicated in March.
Orientation information for Spring admits is communicated in November.
