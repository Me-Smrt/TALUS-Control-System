What is SGA?

Welcome to Tennessee Tech! The Student Government Association (SGA) is here to make
 your university experience the best it can be. Our mission is to protect your rights,
 promote your involvement in campus life, and ensure your voice is heard by the university
 administration. We support a wide range of student activities and encourage personal
 growth, leadership, and community involvement. Whether it's advocating for your needs
 or enhancing your college experience, the SGA is dedicated to serving you and making
 Tennessee Tech a thriving community.

Here are just some of the ways we are promoting positive change at Tech:

Funding Student Organizations: We manage a significant portion of the student activity fee, allocating funds to
 registered student organizations. Have a great idea for a club or event? We can help
 make it happen!

Campus Improvement Projects: From installing more bike racks to upgrading study spaces, we regularly work with
 Facilities to enhance your campus experience.

Academic Advocacy: We've successfully lobbied for extended library hours during finals week and worked
 with faculty to improve course evaluation processes.

Sustainability Initiatives: Our Green Fee has funded modern lighting upgrades in university buildings, provided
 for bottle-fill stations around campus, and expanded recycling programs. We're making
 Tech greener every day!

Mental Health Support: We've partnered with the Counseling Center to increase awareness of mental health
 resources and reduce stigma on campus.

Our commitment is to ensure that every student feels empowered, supported, and heard.
 Whether you're looking to get involved, share your ideas, or simply enjoy the benefits
 of our growing campus community, the SGA is here for you.
