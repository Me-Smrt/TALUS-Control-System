Governing Documents

We, the students of Tennessee Tech University, come together to promote the welfare
 and interests of all students, to provide the necessary elements of responsible self-government,
 and to facilitate cooperation among the university administration, faculty, students,
 and surrounding community. In order to perpetuate an everlasting commitment to these
 good causes, we do hereby proclaim and establish this Constitution of the Student
 Government Association of Tennessee Tech University.

- Preamble to the SGA Constitution

The Tennessee Tech Student Government Association (SGA) operates under a clear set
 of rules and principles outlined in our governing documents. These documents provide
 the essential framework for our structure, procedures, and responsibilities. They
 ensure transparency, accountability, fairness, and consistency in how we represent
 and serve the student body.

Understanding these documents is key to understanding how SGA functions and how students
 can engage with their student government. We are committed to upholding these standards
 and making them accessible to all members of the TTU community.

Core Governing Documents

Below are the primary documents that guide the operations of the TTU SGA. We encourage
 all interested students to familiarize themselves with these foundational texts.

SGA Constitution

Description: This is the supreme governing document of the TTU SGA. It establishes
 the three branches of government (Legislative, Executive, and Judicial), outlines
 their respective powers and duties, defines SGA membership, sets forth fundamental
 procedures, guarantees student rights in relation to SGA, and details the processes
 for elections, amendments, and other core functions. All other SGA policies and actions
 must align with the Constitution.

SGA Policies Manual

Description: This document complements the Constitution by providing more detailed
 operational procedures, rules, and regulations for the day-to-day functioning of SGA.
 It may include items like specifics on committee operations, financial procedures,
 membership requirements details, operational guidelines for initiatives, and other
 procedures not fully elaborated in the Constitution.

Election Guidelines

Description: Governing all SGA-administered elections (including Executive Council,
 Senate, Mr./Ms. TTU, and ballot measures, as per Article III, Sec 5a and Article VIII),
 these guidelines detail the rules for candidacy, campaigning procedures, voting methods
 (preferential or approval-based, per Article VIII, Sec 2), timelines, and the role
 of the Commission for Elections. They ensure fair, transparent, and accessible elections.

Senate Rules of Order

Description: These rules (mentioned in Article II, Sec 2) govern the specific procedures
 of the SGA Senate meetings, including debate, voting, legislative processes, and parliamentary
 procedure, to ensure orderly and efficient conduct of legislative business.

S.O.L.O. Fund Policies & Procedures

Description: While part of the SGA Policies Manual, specific guidelines exist detailing
 the application process, eligibility criteria, review procedures, and allocation rules
 for registered student organizations seeking funding.

Amendments and Updates

Our governing documents are living documents. The SGA Constitution can be amended
 by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the voting membership of the SGA Senate (Article
 X, Section 7). Other documents like the Policies Manual or Election Guidelines typically
 have their own amendment processes, often involving Senate review and approval, ensuring
 they remain relevant and effective.

Questions?

If you have any questions about these governing documents or SGA procedures, please
 don't hesitate to contact the SGA Secretary at sgasecretary@tntech.edu or visit the SGA Office.
