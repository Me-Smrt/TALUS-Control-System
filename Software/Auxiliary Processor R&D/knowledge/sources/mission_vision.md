# Mission and Vision

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: about.

Vision: Tennessee Tech seeks national prominence and impact through engaged students, dedicated faculty, and career-ready graduates known for creativity, tenacity, and analytical problem solving. Mission: Tennessee’s technological university creates, advances, and applies knowledge to expand opportunity and economic competitiveness. It describes itself as a STEM-infused comprehensive institution delivering education, research, and collaborative service. The current mission statement was approved by the Board of Trustees on September 28, 2023.
