Reserved Parking Permits

  All reserved parking spaces went into effect on August 14, 2026.

About Reserved Parking

A Reserved Parking Permit allows you to park in any designated reserved parking space
 within the designated reserved lot associated with your permit. Reserved permits are
 not assigned to a specific parking space. Parking is available on a first-come, first-served
 basis within the reserved area.

Permit Cost

The annual cost is $800.

The permit fee will be deducted through payroll and divided into installments from
 September through May rather than requiring full payment upfront. 

When you are ready to obtain your permit, please contact Parking & Transportation
 Services at parking@tntech.edu or 931-372-6428. Once contacted, we will update your
 AIMS account eligibility, allowing you to purchase a Reserved parking permit.

Reserved Parking Vinyl Cling

Each permit holder receives one reserved parking vinyl cling.

The cling must be displayed on the inside lower-right corner of the rear windshield.

If you drive a different vehicle, transfer the cling before parking in the reserved
 area.

Email parking@tntech.edu with updated license plate information when vehicles change.

Vehicles without a properly displayed cling may be cited and/or towed.

Vinyl clings can be picked up at the Parking & Transportation Office, Room 122, Roaden
 University Center.

Parking Guidelines

Park by pulling forward into space rather than backing in.

Reserved parking is available only within your designated reserved lot.

Reserved permits are valid for one year and must be renewed annually.

Permits are non-transferable.

Unauthorized vehicles may be ticketed and/or towed.

Only employees are eligible to purchase reserved parking permits

How to Request a Reserved Permit or be placed 

Email parking@tntech.edu with the following information:

Name

T-Number

TTU Email Address

Department

Job Title

Preferred Lot Name

Phone Number

Renewals

Current permit holders have priority to renew.

Renewals must be confirmed by July 21 each year.

The annual cost is $800.

Need Assistance?

Report unauthorized vehicles in reserved spaces by contacting our office, 931-372-6428
 or email: parking@tntech.edu.

If an unauthorized vehicle prevents you from parking in the reserved area, call 931-372-6428
 immediately. We can arrange for citation and/or towing and document any alternate
 parking location you are required to use.

Important Notes

Faculty and staff are not required to purchase reserved parking to park on campus.

Regular employee and West/APC Lot parking permits remain available.

Reserved parking is available to employees only.

Availability is not guaranteed.

Reserved spaces are monitored and enforced.
