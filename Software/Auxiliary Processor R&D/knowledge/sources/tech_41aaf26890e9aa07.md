Getting Around Town

UCHRA

The Upper Cumberland Human Resource Agency's Public Transit System provides public transportation to rural residents of all ages.

Go Upper Cumberland     

Go Upper Cumberland is a deviated-fixed route bus system in Cookeville that picks up and drops off passengers
 at designated points along the route.  Tennessee Tech students can ride the Go Upper
 Cumberland buses for free by presenting their student Eagle ID. 

Pick-Up Upper Cumberland

Pick-Up Upper Cumberland is an on-demand transit service in Putnam County. Use the Pick-Up Upper Cumberland
 app to schedule same-day rides, track trips in real time, and pay through your phone.
 All rides are provided by certified, background-checked UCHRA drivers and vehicles.

Connect Upper Cumberland

Connect Upper Cumberland provides daily intercity bus service to Nashville and Murfreesboro, with stops along
 the route—including BNA/Nashville International Airport and other requested destinations.

Ride Upper Cumberland

Ride Upper Cumberland offers door-to-door public transportation with advanced reservations.

Bike Share

Tech’s Bike Share program is funded through the student Green Fee. Not only does this
 provide an efficient way to navigate around campus, but is also an easy way to practice
 sustainability, conserve gas, and encourage physical activity.  

Request Bike

The Bike Share program currently allows students to check out bikes from the Fitness
 Center for a two-week period. With the bicycle, the student is provided with a helmet
 and bike lock. The rental form for the bike package is available through the bottom
 of the Eagle Outdoors' equipment rental form: Bike Share Rental Form

Bike-related amenities on campus include bike racks at all buildings and bike repair
 stands available to students at various locations across campus.

Email us here: bikeshare@tntech.edu 

Bike Repair Stations

Bicycle repair stations are made available to students that offer tire pumps and tools
 for basic maintenance. There are currently 4 on campus:

Capital Quad residence halls (at the center)

North Hall (near the entrance) 

Centennial Plaza (at top of stairs going down to Which Wich!)

Fitness Center (outside the building, at the climbing wall)

Golden Eagles Share the Road

Students, faculty, staff, and visitors share campus roadways, parking lots, sidewalks,
 and crosswalks every day. By staying alert and following safety practices, we can
 create a safer environment for everyone on campus.
