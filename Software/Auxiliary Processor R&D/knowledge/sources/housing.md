# University Housing and Residential Life

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: student_life.

Tennessee Tech Housing and Residential Life manages campus residence halls and Tech Village apartments. The site emphasizes community-based on-campus living and provides applications, housing options, resident resources and FAQs. A new J.J. Oakley Residence Hall is expected to open in Fall 2027. Housing contact information on the public housing pages includes 931-372-3414 and M.S. Cooper Hall Room 218, generally Monday-Friday 8 a.m.-4:30 p.m. Housing availability, rates and application dates change by term.
