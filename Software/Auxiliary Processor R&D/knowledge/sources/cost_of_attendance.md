# 2026-2027 Cost of Attendance

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: costs.

Financial Aid distinguishes direct charges from indirect estimated expenses. The 2026-2027 undergraduate in-state cost-of-attendance example lists $12,685 tuition/fees annually, plus estimated housing, food, transportation, books/supplies and personal expenses. The listed total is $33,656 for a student living on campus and $34,694 for the page’s off-campus/with-parents estimate. Cost of attendance is a financial-aid budget, not necessarily the exact university bill.
