# SACSCOC Accreditation

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: about.

Tennessee Tech University is accredited by the Southern Association of Colleges and Schools Commission on Colleges (SACSCOC) to award baccalaureate, master’s, educational specialist and doctoral degrees, and may offer approved certificates/diplomas at authorized levels. The public SACSCOC page provides official accreditation contact language and links to institutional effectiveness and program inventory resources.
