# Scholarships

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: aid.

For the 2027-2028 academic year, the Tennessee Tech scholarship application is open in this snapshot. The university says one central application can place students in consideration for hundreds of competitive scholarships. The deadline for new freshmen and current students is December 15, 2026. The public site states that new transfer and graduate students admitted for summer/fall 2027 have until June 15, 2027, with January 15 recommended as a priority deadline. Scholarship terms and deadlines should always be treated as time-sensitive.
