Readmission

If you’ve attended Tennessee Tech in the past, and are now returning, you are considered
 a readmission student.  Below you’ll find information on the admission requirements
 for readmission and a step-by-step guide to enrollment.

Admission Requirements

If you left Tennessee Tech on good standing and have not attended any other institution
 since leaving Tech, you fully meet the admission requirements for readmission and
 need only submit an application.

 What if I attended another school after leaving Tech?

Students who have attended elsewhere after leaving Tennessee Tech must meet the following
 readmission requirements:

2.00 GPA in your last full-time semester (or your last 12 hours for part-time students) and

2.00 overall GPA since leaving Tennessee Tech

If you were under probation or suspension (academic or disciplinary) at the institutions
 you attended after leaving Tech, you will not be considered for readmission until
 a satisfactory statement has been furnished by the former college and approval has
 been given by Tennessee Tech’s Admissions Review Committee.

 What if I was suspended from Tennessee Tech?

If you wish to return after a suspension, you’ll need to do the following:

STEP #1: Reapply at www.tntech.edu/apply

STEP #2: You will need to wait 1 full business day for your application to be processed, major and other information to be updated to
 your prior student account. Then, login www.tntech.edu/status for the link to the Readmission After Suspension form to be completed.

Once both of the steps above are completed and the online form is submitted, the form
 automatically goes to the Dean of your major for a decision. Once the Dean has issued a decision
 and signed the form, it is returned to Admissions for processing and then communicated
 to the student. This is typically a 7-10 business day process but may take additional
 days if the staff is in between semesters or away from the University.

If you have questions regarding your academic standing or any part of the suspension
 process, the Office of Admissions is here to help! We’re available at (931) 372-3888
 or admissions@tntech.edu. 

 What if I don't meet the minimum requirements for readmission?

If you do not meet these requirements, a more holistic review will be used to evaluate
 your application for admission. The Admissions Review Committee meets regularly to
 discuss these on a case-by-case basis. If you would like to submit additional documents
 for review, such as a letter of appeal and/or a letter of recommendation from an academic
 advisor or professor, please email these documents to admissions@tntech.edu. 

The review process is a non-competitive, holistic, and individualized process where
 the Review Committee may consider some or all of the following: academic rigor of
 courses, core course GPA and grade trends; as well as non-cognitive elements such
 as an applicant’s concept of self, ability to deal with adversity, long-range goals,
 community involvement, special interests or abilities, leadership, and support structures.
 Requests for review may be sent to admissions@tntech.edu. 

How to Apply

Ready to return to Tennessee Tech? All you’ll need to do is the following:

Step 1: Submit an Application  Fill out our online application at tntech.edu/apply. If you need to make a change to your existing application, please submit an application update form.

Step 2: Request Your Documents If you have attended another institution since leaving Tennessee Tech, you’ll need
 to request official transcripts from every institution be sent to the Admissions office.
 Official transcripts can be mailed to: Office of Undergraduate Admissions, Tennessee Tech University, Campus Box 5006, Cookeville,
 TN. 38505

Transcripts can also be delivered to transcripts@tntech.edu through an approved electronic transcript vendors such as: National Student Clearinghouse, Parchment, Naviance, eScript Safe, Scribs, and JST
