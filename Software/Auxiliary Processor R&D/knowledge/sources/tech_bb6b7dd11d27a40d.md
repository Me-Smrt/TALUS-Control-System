TN Tech Alert is the text messaging notification system designed to notify students,
 faculty and staff about emergencies related to the university. The service is provided
 by Regroup, one of the leading emergency notification companies providing service
 to campuses nationwide. Sign up today for text and e-mail alerts about campus emergencies,
 inclement weather notices, crime alerts and other critical information - sent straight
 to your mobile via our new text messaging service.

Current students who have given the university their cell phone numbers are automatically
 signed up to receive alerts. Faculty, staff, parents, spouses and others interested
 in receiving alerts can sign up as well.

You will be directed to Regroup to sign up for campus and county alerts. 

Register NOW

Existing Users: Instead of creating a new account, sign in using your email and password. If you’ve
 forgotten your password, you can use the “Forgot Password” tool.

New Users: Click the “First Time Users Create Password ” link and follow the on-screen prompts
 to complete your profile. At the conclusion of the sign up process, click the appropriate
 checkboxes beside the TN Tech Alerts list to receive campus alerts.

If you encounter any issues, please fill out the TN Tech Alert Issue Report Form!

Adding Additional Numbers

New students will have their TN Tech Alert username and password sent to their campus
 email account after registration has ended for their first term on campus. Once students
 receive the account creation email, they should promptly login and change their temporary
 password. They can then also change any of their information, including an additional
 family member to their TN Tech Alert account.

Instructions on how to add another mobile phone to your TN Tech Alert account:

Sign into your account; if you’ve forgotten your password, use the “Forgot Password” tool.

Click “Add” to add additional phone numbers or emergency contacts to your account.
 You can also edit or remove existing information by clicking “Edit” or “Remove.”

Obligatory Disclaimer

The TN Tech Alert system is one of several emergency notification systems designed
 to communicate critical information to campus constituents.  Tennessee Tech University
 cannot warrant the successful delivery of each TN Tech Alert message to each individual
 recipient as service depends on the individual e-mail systems, cellular and mobile
 phone carriers to deliver text and e-mail messages to each recipient, which are outside
 the control of Tennessee Tech University.
