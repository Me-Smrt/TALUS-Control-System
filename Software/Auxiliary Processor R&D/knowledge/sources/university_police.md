# University Police and Emergency Information

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: health_safety.

Tennessee Tech University Police operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. For emergencies, call 911. For non-emergency campus police inquiries, call 931-372-3234. The department is in Foundation Hall, 242 East 10th Street. Public resources include emergency alerts, crime reports, active-shooter information, blue-light emergency phones, safety tips and vehicle assistance. Blue-light phones connect directly to the campus emergency line.
