Requirements

Applying to be a student at Tennessee Tech is a quick and seamless process! Processing
 typically takes as little as 3 to 5 business days once all required documents are
 received, depending on time of year. We encourage students to apply early as all students
 must be admitted in order to apply for scholarships. 

Apply Now

Freshman

A freshman is a student who will be graduating soon from high school, or has recently
 graduated, but has not attended college after their high school graduation.

Freshmen Requirements

Transfer

A transfer is a student who has completed college credit after graduating from high
 school. Transfers who have completed less than 24 transferable hours are also evaluated
 by freshman admission requirements.

Transfer Requirements

Readmission

Any student who has completed coursework at Tennessee Tech in the past and wishes
 to return is considered a readmission student. 

Readmission Requirements

Non-Degree Seeking

If you want to take a class for personal enrichment, take a prerequisite for a graduate
 program, pursue an add-on endorsement for a teaching license, or earn a craft certificate,
 you are a non-degree seeking student.

Non-Degree Seeking Requirements

High School Programs

High school programs refer to dual enrollment, joint enrollment, or early admission
 programs in which students currently in high school can pursue college credit. 

High School Program Requirements

Adult Non-Traditional

If you are a new freshman over the age of 21, looking to complete your degree, wishing
 to take a completely or partially online program, or over the age of 65, you may be
 an adult non-traditional student.  

Adult Non-Traditional Requirements
