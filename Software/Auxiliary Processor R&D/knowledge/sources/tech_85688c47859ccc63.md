Full-time Benefited Employees

We strongly encourage all full-time, benefited employees to sign up for payroll deduction
 during the annual enrollment period.  This is a convenient way to reserve your virtual
 parking permit from your computer or mobile device.  The annual enrollment period
 is August 3 to August 31.  The total cost of your virtual parking permit is deducted
 in nine monthly installments (September through May).

ADJUNCT AND HOURLY TEMP PARKING FORM

Important Reminders:

Employees may park in purple and gold spaces.

Employees are not permitted to park in visitor spaces.

Vehicles parked on campus must be registered with a valid parking permit.

Vehicles parked in unauthorized areas or without a valid permit may be subject to
 a citation.

Please ensure your vehicle information remains accurate and up to date in the parking
 portal to avoid receiving a citation.

Please verify that your vehicle is parked in a valid parking space by ensuring it
 is completely between the parking lines and not encroaching on any crosshatched areas.

Remember that signs, painted words on the pavement, and ADA spaces always override
 the general lot designation. If a space is marked with ADA signage, painted restrictions,
 or other parking instructions, do not park there, even if it is located within a Purple
 or Gold Lot.

Please contact the office of Parking & Transportation at parking@tntech.edu or 931-372-6428 with any questions.
