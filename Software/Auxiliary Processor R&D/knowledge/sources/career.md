# Center for Career Development

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: student_services.

The Center for Career Development is Tennessee Tech’s centralized career-planning and student-recruitment center. It supports career planning, resume preparation, job-search skills, employer connections, on-campus interviews, career fairs, and cooperative education. Handshake is the university’s primary job and recruiting gateway for current students and can be reached through TechExpress.
