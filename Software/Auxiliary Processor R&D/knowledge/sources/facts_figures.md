# Facts and Figures

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: about.

The public Facts and Figures page reports Fall 2025 census enrollment of 10,701 students: 9,306 undergraduates and 1,395 graduate students. It reports 91.8% in-state, 5.6% out-of-state, and 2.6% international enrollment. The published student-to-faculty ratio is 16:1 and the average incoming ACT score is 24.2. The page lists large undergraduate programs including Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science, Nursing, Biology, Exercise Science, Civil and Environmental Engineering, Business Management, Electrical Engineering, Psychology, and Elementary Education. Treat these figures as a Fall 2025 snapshot.
