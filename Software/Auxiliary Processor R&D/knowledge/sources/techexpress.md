# TechExpress and ITS Help

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: technology.

TechExpress is Tennessee Tech’s campus portal for one-click access to services such as email, Eagle Online, Handshake and other quick links. Users sign in with their full @tntech.edu email address and use multi-factor authentication. TechExpress is available at express.tntech.edu. ITS Help Desk: helpdesk@tntech.edu, 931-372-3975, generally Monday-Friday 8 a.m.-4:30 p.m. Public ITS articles should be preferred for current login/setup instructions.
