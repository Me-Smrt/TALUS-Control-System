Family Weekend 2026

 September 11 - 13, 2026

Join us for Tennessee Tech’s Family Weekend!

Most events are open to all Tennessee Tech parents and families unless otherwise noted.
 Review the weekend's schedule below to explore various programs and activities while
 visiting your Golden Eagle. This is a special time to connect, explore campus, and
 make memories together! If you have any questions, contact us.
