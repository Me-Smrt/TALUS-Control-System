What to Expect on a VIP Campus Visit!

 What to Expect!

A VIP Campus Visit includes a meeting with an Admissions Counselor, a campus walking
 tour, an academic department meeting*, and a free Tech t-shirt. Also, any registered
 student will be provided a meal voucher to dine on campus. The total time for a visit
 is approximately 3 hours.

*Based on department availability during your scheduled campus visit.
