Eagles Outdoors

*Images of Eagles Outdoors Trips 

Join Eagles Outdoors in exploring the natural wonders of middle Tennessee and beyond.
 Trips, clinics, and adventures are offered throughout the academic year. Tech students
 can join us to go camping, backpacking, climbing, skiing, rafting, biking, kayaking,
 paddle boarding and much more!

Outdoor Equipment Rental

Did you know you can rent outdoor gear from Eagles Outdoors?! The Outdoor Equipment
 Rental program is a resource provided to the Tech community  that is intend to support
 students, staff and faculty to experience the outdoors at their own pace. See our
 wide variety of rental offerings in the drop down menus below.

The Outdoor Equipment Rental Desk located across from the climbing wall in the Marc
 L. Burnett Student Recreation Center.

 Outdoor Equipment Rental Hours (reservations required):

Rental requests must be submitted at least one day prior to requested pick-up date.

 Pick-ups

Monday - Friday: 1 PM - 5 PM

Saturday - Sunday: Closed

 Returns 

Equipment may be returned any time the recreation center is open.

**Bikes, kayaks, canoes, and SUPs must be returned at a prearranged scheduled time.**

 at no cost.

Climbing classes are offered weekly at no cost.
