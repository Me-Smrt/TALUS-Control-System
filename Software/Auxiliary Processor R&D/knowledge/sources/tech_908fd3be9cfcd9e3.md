Local Information

Tennessee Tech University is located within the city of Cookeville, which serves as the county seat for Putnam County. The University's location makes
 it convenient for students to utilize city and county resources close by. The campus
 is less than a mile from a regional hospital and medical complex and approximately
 one mile from two fire departments and Cookeville Police Department.

Students have the accessibility to numerous restaurants, retail, entertainment, and
 recreational opportunities within the city limits. We have compiled a short list of
 local services to help you navigate your student while they are enrolled at Tennessee
 Tech.
