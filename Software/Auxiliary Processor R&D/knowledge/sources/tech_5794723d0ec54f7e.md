Student Parking

The transportation fee paid as part of your student fees now entitles you to a virtual
 parking permit at no additional cost; however, payment of your student fees does not automatically register your vehicle.

Although you do not receive a physical hangtag, students who wish to park on campus are required to claim their virtual permit and
 register their vehicle through the Parking Portal. You will not receive a separate charge for this transaction.

Registering your vehicle helps Parking and Transportation Services maintain accurate
 parking records and identify authorized vehicles on campus.  

Parking & Transportation Services encourages students to become familiar with campus
 parking regulations and designated parking areas before bringing a vehicle on campus.  To get started:

Review the Parking Regulations

Explore the Campus Parking Map to identify where student parking is permitted

2026-2027 Permit Information

2026–2027 Student Parking Permits will be available beginning August 3, 2026. Please
 complete and update your vehicle registration by August 14, 2026, for the Fall semester
 and by January 14, 2027, for the Spring semester.  If your unsure of the permit ordering
 process, here is how to order parking permit in the parking portal.  Students may register multiple vehicles to their account; however, only one registered
 vehicle may be parked on campus at any given time.  You will know your vehicle registration
 has been successfully issued when you receive a confirmation receipt.

 Important Reminders:

Students may only park in areas designated for student parking.

Students are not permitted to park in visitor spaces.

Vehicles parked on campus must be registered with a valid parking permit.

Vehicles parked in unauthorized areas or without a valid permit may be subject to
 a citation.

Please ensure your vehicle information remains accurate and up to date in the parking portal to avoid receiving a citation.

Please verify that your vehicle is parked in a valid parking space by ensuring it
 is completely between the parking lines and not encroaching on any crosshatched areas.

Remember that signs, painted words on the pavement, and ADA spaces always override
 the general lot designation. If a space is marked with ADA signage, painted restrictions,
 or other parking instructions, do not park there, even if it is located within a Purple
 Lot.

Understanding campus parking policies and parking locations helps ensure a safe, convenient, and accessible parking experience for the entire
 campus community.
