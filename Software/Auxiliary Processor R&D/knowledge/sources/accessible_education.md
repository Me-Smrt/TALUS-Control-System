# Accessible Education Center

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: student_services.

The Accessible Education Center works to ensure equal access to academic and physical environments for qualifying individuals, including academic adjustments, assistive technology/software and related support. Office: Roaden University Center 112. Phone 931-372-6119; email disability@tntech.edu. Public office hours are Monday-Friday 8 a.m.-noon and 1-4:30 p.m. Accommodation eligibility and documentation are case-specific and should be handled through the AEC.
