# Housing Applications and Timing

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: student_life.

Spring 2027 residence-hall applications for new students are scheduled to open October 1, 2026 at 10 a.m. Central. The page lists a $200 prepayment at application time, assignments based on application date, and term-specific application/roommate deadlines. Fall 2027 and Spring 2028 housing are handled through the academic-year application. Rates are approved annually and vary by term and room/apartment type.
