# Health Services

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: health_safety.

Health Services is located in Bell Hall 105 and provides acute/urgent care, preventive care, immunizations, STI treatment, physicals, laboratory testing and allergy injections for registered students and faculty/staff. Public hours are Monday-Friday 8 a.m.-4:30 p.m. Central. Contact: 931-372-3320, healthservices@tntech.edu. The site directs new students to review current immunization requirements.
