# Colleges and Schools

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: academics.

Academic units include the College of Agriculture and Human Ecology; College of Arts and Sciences; College of Business; College of Education and Human Sciences; College of Emerging and Integrative Studies; College of Engineering; College of Fine Arts; College of Graduate Studies; and the Whitson-Hester School of Nursing. The university also highlights health sciences and pre-professional tracks, Honors, Army ROTC, and Volpe Library as important academic resources.
