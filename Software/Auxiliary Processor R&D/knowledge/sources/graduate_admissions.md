# Graduate Admissions

Snapshot date: 2026-09-29. Category: admissions.

The College of Graduate Studies publishes separate deadlines for domestic/non-degree and international applicants. The page lists a $35 graduate application fee and $40 for international graduate applicants, transcripts, and any program-specific items such as test scores or recommendation letters. International deadlines occur earlier than domestic deadlines. Counseling and Psychology programs are noted as fall-admit-only on the public page. Program-specific admission criteria should be checked in the graduate catalog.
