Visitor Parking

Visitor parking is exclusively reserved for guests and is not available to faculty, staff, or students. Virtual visitor permits allow guests to park in any visitor space on campus. If all
 visitor parking spaces are occupied, Parking and Transportation Services can assist
 visitors in obtaining an alternate parking space. Stop by the Parking and Transportation
 Office in Roaden University Center 122 in the main lobby, call 931-372-6428, or email
 parking@tntech.edu for assistance.

You can scan the QR code below to obtain a virtual visitor permit. Permits are also
 available through the Parking Portal or in person at the Parking & Transportation
 Services office. Be sure to have your license plate number ready to ensure a smooth
 and efficient process.

Departmental Visitors

Each department has designated employees who are authorized to send guest parking
 invitations on behalf of their area. To identify your department's authorized contact
 for Departmental Guest Invites, please contact your department dean or supervisor,
 or email parking@tntech.edu for assistance.

Departmental Guest Permit Instructions »

These permits are valid for up to 5 days.

A guest invite may be sent no earlier than 30 days before the scheduled visit.

To request a permit, you will need:

 Guest's name

 Email address

 Date(s) of visit

Suggested comments to add to Departmental Guest Invites: Upon your arrival on campus,
 please complete your invite. The permit allows access to visitor and employee parking
 spaces, but does not permit parking in reserved spaces. For a campus map, please visit
 https://www.tntech.edu/maps/index.php.

The guest will receive an email invitation from tennesseetech@parking-receipt.com.

They must click the link provided in the email and fill out the requested information.

Under "Contact Information," the guest should enter their first name, last name, email
 address, and phone number.

Under "Vehicle," the guest needs to click “Add Vehicle,” enter the license plate number,
 select the issuing state, and provide the make, model, and color of the vehicle. After
 completing these steps, they should click “Add” and then select “Complete Guest Permit
 Invite.”

Upon finishing the process, the guest will receive a receipt indicating a zero-dollar
 charge.

To create an invite batch for multiple guests:

Enter the event name in the designated field for the batch name.

Copy and paste the individual email addresses into the “Emails for all invitees” field.

Enter one email address per line to ensure each invite is sent correctly.  This will
 make sure all individuals receive the invite.

Please note: If your guest is a current employee, student, or has a license plate already registered
 to an employee or student, their request will be automatically declined when they
 attempt to complete the guest invitation.  You will need to contact parking@tntech.edu
 or call 931-372-6428 to assist this guest with a permit.

AIMS Validation Pass:

The AIMS Validation Pass is a one day permit only for large events, camps, and tours.
 Please contact the Parking Department to obtain the QR code, URL, and voucher code
 for your visitors. You will need to provide the following details about your event
 to Parking:

 Event name

 Date

 Time

 Location

Upon arrival, guests can scan the QR code or click the URL, then enter their license
 plate number, state of issuance, and voucher code. They will receive an on-screen
 confirmation of their free parking and the permits expiration date.  This pass allows
 access to visitor and employee spaces, but does not permit parking in reserved spaces. 

Fitness Center Visitor Parking

There are fourteen visitor parking spots in front of the Student Recreation and Fitness
 Center.  These spots are strictly for visitors who are not faculty, staff, or students. These spaces have a two-hour limit and are intended solely for visitors to the Fitness
 Center.  

Should you have additional questions, call Parking Services at 931-372-6428.
