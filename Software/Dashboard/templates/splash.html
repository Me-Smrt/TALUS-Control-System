One robot, layered authority.

01 / CONTROL

Deterministic robotics

Raspberry Pi orchestration, CCB supervision, point-to-point CAN, local RCB watchdogs, and explicit safety boundaries.

02 / PERCEPTION

Human motion tracking

Face, gaze, eyelids, hands, body pose, retargeting, and InMoov inverse kinematics with Blender visualization.

Replaceable AI node

An auxiliary accelerator handles scene understanding, local AI, speech recognition, and speech synthesis without owning actuators.
